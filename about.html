About Mixology

Mixology is a social media platform for drink enthusiasts to share their recipes with other passionate individuals. Our platform allows users to create and share their own drink recipes, as well as discover new recipes from other users. Users can also create collections of their favorite recipes and follow other users to stay updated on their latest posts. Mixology is the perfect place for drink enthusiasts to connect with others who share their passion for mixology.
