Shirley Temple

A Shirley Temple is a non-alcoholic cocktail named after the famous child actress Shirley Temple. It is a sweet and refreshing drink that is popular among both children and adults. The drink typically consists of ginger ale or lemon-lime soda mixed with grenadine syrup and garnished with a maraschino cherry. Sometimes, a splash of orange juice is also added to enhance the flavor.

Ingredients:

  • lemon-lime soda
  • grenadine
  • maraschino cherry
