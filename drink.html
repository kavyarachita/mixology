Matcha Latte

Category: Tea

Ingredients:

  • Matcha Powder
  • Water
  • Milk

Instructions:

Heat your water up to 78 degrees Celsius. Sift your matcha powder into a bowl. Pour hot water over powder and whisk thorughly until bubbles form. Fill a class with ice, milk, and a sweetner of your choice. Pour the whisked matcha over and enjoy!
